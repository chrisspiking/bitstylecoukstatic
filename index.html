About

Small on purpose

BitStyle.co.uk is my independent development practice, based in the UK. I've spent my working life building for the web, and I started BitStyle to do it the way I think it should be done — directly, transparently, and with the person writing the code actually in the room.

That means you get one point of contact who understands the whole system. Nothing gets lost in a handover, because there isn't one. It also means I take on a small number of projects at a time, so the work I'm doing for you gets proper attention.

How I work

  1. Understand first

    Before any code, I want to know what the business needs and what success actually looks like. Often the brief changes at this point — for the better.

  2. Fixed scope, clear price

    You get a written scope and a fixed price before we start. If something needs to change mid-project, we agree it in writing first.

  3. Build in the open

    Work goes to a staging URL early and updates as it progresses. You see real progress, not status reports.

  4. Stay available

    Launch isn't the end of the relationship. I keep things patched, monitored and improving — and I'm still the person who answers.
